## 0.2.0 — History-conditioned safe-return research stack

This release moves DynNav from the original risk/recoverability scaffold to a publication-facing history-conditioned safe-return research stack.

### Research core

- action-triggered stochastic topology-hazard model with persistent activation history;
- exact future-closure safe-return probability oracle;
- exact augmented-state history-aware planner;
- critical-cut approximation with tested scaling regime and explicit joint-cut counterexample;
- hard safe-return and state-only marginal baselines;
- paired common-random-number execution benchmarks and statistical utilities;
- held-out probability/horizon and frozen geometric-topology evaluations;
- soft-history vs hard-safe-return Pareto sweep;
- IEEE-format manuscript with machine-readable evidence manifest.

### ROS 2 / Nav2

- C++17 `nav2_core::GlobalPlanner` integration on ROS 2 Jazzy;
- persistent hazard history updated from executed transitions rather than planned paths;
- ROS/Gazebo benchmark infrastructure and frozen action-triggered execution protocol;
- CI build, plugin discovery and configuration checks.

### Reproducibility and repository quality

- Python 3.10 / 3.11 / 3.12 CI matrix;
- retained evidence/provenance contracts;
- paper build and unresolved-reference checks;
- documentation inventory and link validation;
- website and researcher-workspace build/audit checks;
- full-main workflow audit support;
- canonical repository/evidence guide and explicit claim boundaries.

### Current limitations

- history-conditioned action-triggered Gazebo efficacy outcomes are not yet retained publication evidence;
- closure probabilities are model inputs, not calibrated real-world probabilities;
- partial-observability and probability-miscalibration stress tests remain future work;
- no physical-robot efficacy or safety-certification claim is made.
