# Hostile Review and Resolution Log

Review date: 2026-09-19. This review evaluates the narrow publication claim in
`paper/dynnav_r/main.tex`; it does not treat repository size as scientific
evidence.

## Reviewer A — motion planning and robotics

### Fatal concerns

- **The execution evidence is not yet sufficient for a robotics efficacy
  claim.** The C++ Nav2 plugin and frozen action-triggered Gazebo protocol are
  implementation evidence, while the retained dynamic Gazebo run is only
  `n=1` commissioning and does not contain an irreversibility event.
  **Classification:** requires new experiment. **Resolution:** the manuscript
  makes no Gazebo-efficacy or hardware claim; the frozen paired workflow remains
  an explicit gate.

### Major concerns

- **A grid-connectivity oracle omits dynamics, localization error, perception
  delay, footprint constraints, and controller failure.** **Classification:**
  fundamental limitation of the current model. **Resolution:** explicit in the
  manuscript and claim matrix.
- **Three hand-authored topologies are not broad geometric generalization.**
  **Classification:** requires new experiment. **Resolution:** wording is
  restricted to mechanism replication in three frozen synthetic worlds.
- **Hard safe-return planning may be as effective as the soft objective.**
  **Classification:** fixed now. **Resolution:** retained as a negative result;
  the paper does not claim universal soft-objective superiority.

### Minor concerns

- ROS integration tests do not establish real-time performance or deployment
  safety. **Classification:** fixed now by claim boundary.

## Reviewer B — probabilistic planning and algorithms

### Fatal concerns

- **The method is classical state augmentation unless the contribution is
  stated more narrowly.** **Classification:** fixed now. **Resolution:** the
  paper claims an action-triggered return-topology formulation and a measured
  state-only information gap, not invention of state augmentation, MDPs, or
  history-dependent planning.

### Major concerns

- **The full path history is not the minimal state.** The implementation uses
  the activated-hazard set, a sufficient statistic under the stated model.
  **Classification:** fixed now. **Resolution:** the manuscript defines the
  augmented state as `(position, activated hazards)` and uses “history” only to
  describe how that statistic is generated.
- **Independent Bernoulli closures are restrictive.** Equal marginals can be
  misleading under correlation. **Classification:** fundamental limitation / 
  requires model extension. **Resolution:** the limitation and a correlated
  scenario counterexample are retained; online correlated support is not
  claimed.
- **Exact enumeration is exponential.** **Classification:** fundamental
  computational limitation. **Resolution:** scaling is measured, a faster
  critical-cut approximation is included, and its joint-cut optimism is
  explicitly falsified.

### Minor concerns

- A* optimality applies only to the declared augmented-state objective and
  admissible geometric heuristic, not to safety or mission utility in general.
  **Classification:** fixed now by scope.

## Reviewer C — experimental methodology and reproducibility

### Fatal concerns

- **Synthetic success rates cannot be presented as real-world probabilities.**
  **Classification:** fixed now. **Resolution:** probabilities are model inputs;
  calibration, hardware efficacy, and certification are unsupported.

### Major concerns

- **Planner comparisons must be paired even when activated event subsets
  differ.** **Classification:** fixed now. **Resolution:** common latent draws
  are keyed by seed, scenario, repetition, and event identity; binary analyses
  use paired risk differences, paired bootstrap intervals, and exact McNemar
  tests.
- **A passing test suite previously omitted ROS-independent benchmark
  contracts.** **Classification:** fixed now. **Resolution:** malformed tests
  were repaired and added to default pytest discovery.
- **Timing results are machine-dependent and not population estimates.**
  **Classification:** fixed now. **Resolution:** timings are labelled retained
  representative measurements and are not promoted to universal performance.
- **The evidence manifest records older workflow revisions.**
  **Classification:** requires release procedure. **Resolution:** published
  numerical claims remain tied to their retained generating artifacts; the
  final submission release must freeze and tag a clean commit rather than
  silently relabel old artifacts as generated by new code.

### Minor concerns

- Claim-scanning warnings include scientifically appropriate negations such as
  “not a formal safety guarantee.” **Classification:** tool limitation. These
  warnings require human review and must not be “fixed” by deleting disclaimers.

## Second-pass decision

No remaining criticism justifies strengthening the claims. Fixable repository
inconsistencies identified above have been corrected. The remaining major gates
are new action-triggered execution evidence, broader predeclared evaluation,
probability-miscalibration/delayed-revelation studies, and final release-level
bibliography and provenance checks. Until those gates are satisfied, the
submission decision remains the one in `PUBLICATION_READINESS.md`.
