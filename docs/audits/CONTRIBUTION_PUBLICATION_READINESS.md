# Contribution Publication Readiness

## Primary thesis track

C00 has the clearest research coherence: safe-return probability conditioned on action-triggered future topology hazards and executed-action history. The exact small-world oracle and augmented-state planner form a defensible narrow study. Publication readiness still depends on independent held-out topologies, fair baselines, scaling/approximation analysis, matched stochastic trials, and functioning ROS 2/Gazebo execution. Historical review flags risk/recoverability confounding and incomplete provenance, which remain relevant.
