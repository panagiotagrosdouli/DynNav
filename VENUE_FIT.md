# Venue Fit

DynNav is currently strongest as a focused planning/robotics paper about information-state representation under action-triggered topology hazards. It is not presently supported as a deployment-safety, physical-robot, or broad autonomous-systems validation paper.

| Venue category | Current fit | Additional evidence normally needed |
|---|---|---|
| Robotics planning workshop or focused symposium | Plausible now after release freeze | Clean tagged reproduction, final citation audit, concise positioning against history-dependent risk and belief-state planning. |
| Main-track robotics conference | Conditional | Retained paired action-triggered simulation runs, broader predeclared topology families, probability-miscalibration and delayed-revelation studies, and clearer runtime/memory limits. |
| Autonomous-systems or field-robotics venue | Weak at present | Multiple realistic simulation environments and preferably named-platform physical trials with failures and exclusions retained. |
| Algorithms/probabilistic-planning venue | Conditional | Stronger formal treatment of the minimal sufficient statistic, correlation/partial-observability extensions or lower bounds, and comparison with an explicit MDP/POMDP formulation. |
| Safety/verification venue | Not ready | Formal specifications and proofs or verified runtime monitors. Existing return-probability tests and hard constraints are not safety certification. |
| Journal extension | Future option | A substantially broader evaluation, full ablations, validated ROS/Gazebo execution, calibration analysis, and ideally hardware evidence; it must add more than repository scale. |

No acceptance probability is implied. Venue selection should follow the final evidence scope rather than drive stronger claims.
